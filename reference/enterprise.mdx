<Note>
  Running high volume? Open the [pricing page](https://flatkey.ai/pricing), find the **Enterprise** plan, and select **Contact sales**. Enterprise contracts cover committed-volume discounts, custom routing, invoicing, and procurement.
</Note>

Self-serve plans cover most individual and small-team workloads. An Enterprise contract makes sense once your volume, payment process, or procurement requirements outgrow them.

## Contracts and billing

<Accordion title="When should I move to an Enterprise contract?">
  Consider an Enterprise contract when your usage outgrows the self-serve plans, when your team needs invoicing instead of card payments, or when procurement requires a signed agreement. If you are unsure, share your monthly token volume and target models with [support@flatkey.ai](mailto:support@flatkey.ai).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does an Enterprise contract include?">
  Enterprise pricing is custom. Contracts cover committed-volume discounts, custom routing, invoicing, and procurement support. Exact terms depend on your volume and model mix — see the **Enterprise** plan on the [pricing page](https://flatkey.ai/pricing).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Flatkey offer invoice-based billing?">
  Yes. Enterprise accounts support invoice billing, custom monthly usage, team procurement, and dedicated routing discounts. Contact [support@flatkey.ai](mailto:support@flatkey.ai) to discuss terms.
</Accordion>

## Teams and access control

<Accordion title="Can several people share one Enterprise account?">
  Yes. Issue a separate API key per person or per service, then use **Groups** to apply model access policies and spend quotas to each key. Every request appears in **Usage Logs** with its key, so you can attribute cost per team or per project.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I restrict which models a key can access?">
  Yes. Use **Groups** in the console to set model access policies and spend quotas on individual keys or groups of keys.
</Accordion>

## Security review

<Accordion title="What do you need for a vendor security review?">
  Send your questionnaire to [support@flatkey.ai](mailto:support@flatkey.ai). Flatkey's retention and compliance posture is summarized under [Privacy and security](/reference/faq#privacy-and-security) in the FAQ.
</Accordion>
